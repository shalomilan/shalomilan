### Hi, I'm Ilán

Biologist and data scientist working where **wildlife ecology meets machine learning**.

- **Ecology:** BSc + MSc-equivalent in Biological Sciences (University of Buenos Aires), specialised in animal ecology. My research used camera traps to study how **invasive mammals** (axis deer, wild boar) overlap in space and time with native fauna in a national park in Argentina.
- **Publication:** Shalom, I. et al. (2024). *Do exotic invasive mammals disturb the native fauna? Spatiotemporal distribution and overlap between species in a national park of Argentina.* **Integrative Zoology**. [doi:10.1111/1749-4877.12848](https://doi.org/10.1111/1749-4877.12848)
- **Industry:** Business Analyst at Cervecería y Maltería Quilmes (AB InBev). I turn business questions into data products:
  - Built and maintain a **daily automated report for directors** that supports country-level decisions, tracking cumulative task-validation rates with interactive filters
  - Maintain a **shared analytical table** (month × customer × task) used by several teams as their source of truth for task validation
  - SQL and PySpark pipelines on Databricks and Azure Synapse; Power BI semantic models (DAX, DirectQuery)

#### What I'm working on
Applying ML and spatial modelling to **biological invasions**, starting with forecasting the cane toad invasion front in Australia, and building open Python tools for invasion ecology.

#### Toolbox
`Python` (pandas, scikit-learn, GeoPandas, PySpark) · `R` (tidyverse, overlap, camtrapR) · `SQL` · `Databricks` · `Power BI` · `QGIS` · `Git`
